# Common benchmark dataset screen

**Scope.** This screen follows the user's narrowed direction: assess the commonly used benchmark datasets for the requested supervised tasks, not every dataset discoverable online. A candidate is retained only when the exact route is (1) suitable for fixed-width tabular MLP inputs and the named supervised target, (2) anonymously downloadable without a registration/agreement gate, (3) covered by a reuse license acceptable for open reuse, (4) feasible with the repository's one-full-training-partition batch and largest `[512]*5` vanilla MLP on a 6 GiB GPU, and (5) empirically below saturation on a leakage-safe split. A public page or an open-source loader does not establish dataset rights. “Retain provisionally” means the recorded pilot supports only the exact task, split, seed, model sizes and training budget reported.

The source-level inventory and detailed per-candidate evidence remain in [the larger dataset audit](OPEN_SUPERVISED_TABULAR_DNN_DATASETS.md). This focused screen summarizes the mainstream families and points to those exact records. Novelty detection is out of scope; labeled event classification remains in scope.

## Time-series forecasting

| Common benchmark | Compatibility and evidence | Gate decision |
|---|---|---|
| ETT (ETTh1, ETTh2, ETTm1, ETTm2) | Standard multivariate lookback-to-horizon arrays flatten directly to dense MLP input. The upstream [ETDataset repository](https://github.com/zhouhaoyi/ETDataset) is public and the dataset files are directly retrievable, but its [LICENSE](https://github.com/zhouhaoyi/ETDataset/blob/main/LICENSE) is CC BY-ND 4.0. A separate curated [Time-Series-Library Hub card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/thuml/Time-Series-Library) labels its aggregate repository CC BY 4.0; that label does not resolve whether the original ETT provider authorized relicensing. | **Cross out pending rights clarification.** Under the upstream route, ND conflicts with this benchmark's derived-window/adaptation and redistribution requirement. No MLP difficulty pilot was used to override that rights issue. |
| Electricity / ECL (the common 321-series, hourly benchmark) | Numeric load histories are MLP-compatible, but the audited source route did not pin an authoritative payload and license for this exact 321-series/hourly artifact. This benchmark must not be conflated with the UCI 370-client, 15-minute ElectricityLoadDiagrams archive. | **Cross out** pending source-level artifact and rights verification. See the exact ECL row in the OpenTS-Bench screen. |
| UCI ElectricityLoadDiagrams 2011–2014 (370 clients, 15-minute) | The [UCI record](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/321/electricityloaddiagrams20112014) states CC BY 4.0. For 128-step lookback and one-step targets, there are 51,847,360 possible windows before splitting; a 70% chronological prefix gives about 36,278,870 training windows. `[512]*5` hidden activations alone would use about 346 GiB; the dense input matrix would take about 6.25 TiB. The official ZIP endpoint was reachable but ignored the byte-range request; the full payload/parser was not completed in that screen. | **Cross out the pooled benchmark route** for full-batch scale. Do not replace it with a per-meter task and call it the same benchmark. See K2 and the UCI Electricity entry in the main audit. |
| Traffic / PEMS-BAY, PEMS04, PEMS08 | Fixed-window numeric MLPs are architecturally valid. [Caltrans says PeMS use requires applying for an account](https://dot.ca.gov/programs/traffic-operations/mpr/pems-source). Common community mirrors do not independently establish unrestricted rights to redistribute the source data. | **Cross out** as no-gate/open-rights failures. A mirror or model-library loader is not a license. |
| Weather | Numeric fixed-window forecasting is MLP-compatible. The commonly used benchmark mirror/source route did not establish payload provenance and reuse license in the audited source record. | **Cross out** for unresolved source rights/provenance; no difficulty pilot. |
| Exchange | Numeric multivariate forecasting is compatible in shape, but the common benchmark route has no pinned payload/license in the screened source. Its small sample count also makes a robust large-model difficulty claim weak without task-level validation. | **Cross out** for unverified source rights and insufficient evidence. |
| ILI | Weekly numeric prediction is compatible in shape; the canonical benchmark is short and has no pinned open artifact/license in the screened route. | **Cross out** pending a source-verified route and empirical headroom check. |
| Monash Australian Electricity Demand (horizons 1, 7, 28) | Open CC BY 4.0 artifact, anonymous download and full provider parsing verified. Full-batch vanilla `[16,16]` and `[128,128]` runs for 30 epochs remain far from saturation at all three horizons, but R² is below -1.9 and absolute forecast skill is poor. | **Retain provisionally as a hard stress task, not a strong forecasting benchmark.** Exact split and metrics are in the main audit. |

## Fixed-window sequence prediction / labeling

| Common benchmark | Compatibility and evidence | Gate decision |
|---|---|---|
| UCR / UEA time-series archives | Fixed-length series can be flattened for a feed-forward MLP; this is sequence-to-label classification, not a recurrent model. The archive is not one uniformly licensed dataset: rights and archive routes vary by constituent. | **Do not admit the archive wholesale.** The supplemental audit records per-dataset access/parsing and representative pilots; an individual task must pass its own rights and difficulty checks. |
| UCI HAR (raw inertial windows) | CC BY 4.0; official raw archive downloaded and parsed. A 128-sample, six-channel flattened input fits a dense MLP. Subject-held-out test performance stays below 88% accuracy and below .868 macro-F1 for tested `[128,128]` and `[512]*5` models. The precomputed 561-feature route is excluded because upstream normalization provenance is unresolved. | **Retain provisionally** for raw-window activity classification only. It is not evidence for per-timestep labeling. |
| UCI HAPT | CC BY 4.0 source; fixed sensor windows and per-sample transition labels are structurally compatible with a dense MLP. The subject-disjoint pilot reached accuracy .514 and macro-F1 .259, but boundary-F1 was only .00128, indicating that the tested MLP essentially failed to identify transition boundaries. | **Cross out as a useful sequence-label benchmark for this MLP setup.** The unsaturated result reflects near-failure on the temporal boundary target, not meaningful headroom. See the exact pilot in the main audit. |
| PAMAP2, MHEALTH, OPPORTUNITY, HARTH and HHAR | These established activity corpora have individually screened source payloads and window/task definitions in the main audit. Each is a distinct subject/session split; do not merge them or infer per-step sequence-label evidence from a sequence-to-one window classifier. Recorded pilots are below ceiling, with varying class imbalance and generalization limits. | **Retain only the exact provisional routes already recorded** in the main audit; other sensor/device/window variants remain untested or crossed out there. |

## Supervised event / physiological sequence tasks

| Common benchmark | Compatibility and evidence | Gate decision |
|---|---|---|
| MIT-BIH Arrhythmia | Open Access, ODC-By 1.0, public anonymous files; beat-centered numeric ECG windows fit the dense MLP. Record-disjoint pilots for three widths are far from ceiling; macro-F1 exposes substantial class-imbalance difficulty. | **Retain provisionally** for the recorded beat-classification target and split. See the main audit's PhysioNet provider evidence. |
| MIT-BIH Supraventricular Arrhythmia (SVDB) | ODC Attribution 1.0; all 417 official objects were anonymously acquired and checksum-verified. One-second two-lead windows fit; record-disjoint `[16,16]`, `[128,128]`, `[512]*5` pilots improve test AP from .315 to .481 against .093 prevalence, without saturation. The split is record-disjoint, not proven patient-disjoint. | **Retain provisionally** for the exact `S` versus `N` beat task; do not generalize to other beat-label mappings. |
| Apnea-ECG | Open Access, ODC-By 1.0 and direct anonymous download. One-minute windows fit the MLP and test accuracy remains about .60, but wider models collapse to majority predictions and balanced accuracy is near .50. | **Cross out as a useful benchmark for this setup** because the pilot shows weak discrimination, despite no ceiling saturation. Keep its result as rejection evidence. |
| Daphnet freezing-of-gait | CC BY 4.0, official anonymous archive and numeric windows; however, the subject-held-out pilot collapses toward majority predictions and weighted runs are unstable. Published high scores use different protocols. | **Cross out** as unreliable under the screened subject-held-out setup. |
| Sleep-EDF Expanded | Open Access, ODC-BY 1.0; epoch-wise sleep-stage labels and flattened EEG are structurally MLP-compatible. Native-rate batch and the tested one-channel downsampled full-batch path exceed the GPU memory budget. | **Cross out** for the required full-batch/maximum-model profile. |

## Supervised learning-to-rank

| Common benchmark | Compatibility and evidence | Gate decision |
|---|---|---|
| LETOR / MQ2007 / MQ2008 | Numeric query-document feature rows and grouped NDCG are a direct dense-MLP ranking formulation. The official distribution uses an agreement-controlled route; no unrestricted primary artifact/license was verified. | **Cross out as gated/uncleared.** |
| MSLR-WEB10K / WEB30K | Numeric 136-feature query-document examples are ideal MLP input, but the official Microsoft route requires accepting its agreement. | **Cross out as gated.** |
| Yahoo Learning to Rank Challenge | Query-grouped click/relevance is compatible with an MLP scorer, but challenge terms control distribution and use; no unrestricted primary license/download was verified. | **Cross out as terms-controlled.** |
| WANDS | MIT-licensed public release; query-product relevance rows are parsed and anonymous download verified. Query-group split and full-batch MLP pilots were run. Structured-only inputs produce majority predictions and below-random held-out ranking; headroom appears only after adding lexical overlap features derived from text, outside this tabular-only input contract. | **Cross out** for lack of a useful non-saturated task using allowed structured tabular features. The text-derived result is rejection evidence, not an admitted route. |

## Supervised recommendation

| Common benchmark | Compatibility and evidence | Gate decision |
|---|---|---|
| MovieLens (100K / 1M / 20M / latest) | Rating prediction is a valid scalar MLP task, but an unrestricted open-data license is not established by the source terms; GroupLens states use restrictions. Larger releases also exceed the dense one-hot full-batch profile. | **Cross out** for rights and, for larger releases, full-batch capacity. |
| Amazon Reviews (2018 / 2023) | Rating regression after dropping text is structurally MLP-compatible. Official pages provide user/item IDs and ratings; all individually screened 2023 category links were anonymously range-accessible, while all 2018 ratings-only endpoint probes timed out. The data rights are not openly licensed; the 2018 source requests research-only use. Larger categories exceed dense-ID/full-batch limits. | **Cross out** for rights; scale additionally excludes most categories. The 2023 `Subscription_Boxes` category remains too small/unlicensed to establish useful headroom. |
| Yelp Open Dataset | Rating/review-derived tasks can be tabularized, but the official user agreement controls access and restricts use; interaction logs do not provide complete exposed non-click candidates. | **Cross out** for agreement-controlled rights and task semantics. |
| MIND (large / small) | Logged impressions contain clicked and non-clicked candidates, so structured click prediction is MLP-compatible if text is omitted. Microsoft Research License Terms gate the research download; full variant also fails batch/ID scale. | **Cross out as gated.** |
| Criteo / Avazu CTR | Binary click targets and fixed tabular fields suit an MLP, but the competition-derived releases have no verified unrestricted data license and contain 40M+ rows; `[512]*5` full-batch activation floors exceed device capacity by orders of magnitude. | **Cross out** for rights and full-batch scale. |
| KuaiRec | Fully observed user-video ratings could support watch-ratio regression; anonymous Zenodo byte-range access was verified. However, the deposit has no dataset Rights/License value, and a dense one-hot full batch is vastly beyond the device memory budget. | **Cross out** for unresolved dataset rights and full-batch scale. |
| Goodbooks-10k | Public ratings archive is downloadable, but repository code licensing does not establish the Goodreads-derived data's rights. Its 5.98M ratings also imply huge dense one-hot input and activation tensors; no canonical timestamp split exists in the ratings table. | **Cross out** for rights, scale and split limitations. |
| RecoReact | Public CC-licensed files and anonymous download; however, its 1,785 examples measure whole-slate satisfaction, not candidate-item relevance, and the cohort is too small to demonstrate a difficult unsaturated benchmark. | **Cross out** for target mismatch and inadequate scale. |

## Result under the narrowed scope

- **Provisionally retained:** Monash Australian Electricity Demand (stress-only, weak fit); UCI HAR raw windows; exact individually piloted PAMAP2, MHEALTH, OPPORTUNITY, HARTH and HHAR routes; MIT-BIH Arrhythmia; and the exact SVDB `S`-versus-`N` route. The HAPT per-step route is crossed out because its boundary-F1 is effectively zero.
- **No ranking or recommendation benchmark currently passes all gates.** The most common candidates are agreement-gated, rights-unclear, beyond the full-batch memory profile, too small/easy, or only useful with text-derived inputs.
- **Cross out** all other named common candidates above for their recorded reason. “Provisional” is not final qualification: it means that evidence covers only the stated splits and tested MLPs, not every vanilla width/depth or ADP variant.
- This screen is the deliberately bounded common-benchmark set. It is not a claim of an internet-wide census, and it does not promote a public download mirror into an open license.
